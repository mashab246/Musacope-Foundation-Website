Our 2026 Impact Report: A Year of Transformation

📅 July 15, 2026 ✍️ Musacope Team ⏱️ 5 min read
📊

2026 has been a landmark year for Musacope Foundation. Thanks to the extraordinary generosity of our donors, the tireless effort of our volunteers, and the trust of the communities we serve, we've reached more people than ever before.

This report is our commitment to transparency and gratitude — a snapshot of what we achieved together this year, told honestly and openly.

By the Numbers

  • 12,000+ lives directly impacted across 8 districts
  • 1,200 students supported with scholarships and learning materials
  • 3,400 free medical consultations at 22 outreach camps
  • 45 community projects completed or in progress
  • 200 women trained in vocational skills and business
  • 2 new boreholes serving 1,200 people daily
"Before Musacope, my daughter walked 4 kilometers for water. Today, she walks 4 minutes — and uses those extra hours to study. That is what your foundation has given us."

— Nakato, community member, Teso region

Where Every Dollar Went

Financial accountability is not optional — it's foundational to who we are. Here's how every dollar donated in 2026 was allocated:

  • 68% — Direct program delivery (education, health, WASH, livelihoods)
  • 18% — Community outreach and field operations
  • 9% — Administration, compliance, and audit
  • 5% — Fundraising and awareness campaigns

What We've Learned

This year taught us something we already believed but now know more deeply: the most powerful change comes when communities lead their own transformation. Every project we funded in 2026 was co-designed with local leaders. Every intervention respected local knowledge. And every long-term win — from girls staying in school to families opening businesses — belongs to the people themselves.

Looking Ahead to 2027

Next year, we're doubling down on what works. Our 2027 priorities include:

  1. Launching our first vocational training center in Kampala
  2. Expanding scholarships to 2,000 students
  3. Opening a maternal health clinic in Karamoja
  4. Scaling our women's micro-enterprise program to 3 new districts

None of this happens without you. Thank you for making 2026 possible.

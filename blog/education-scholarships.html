50 New Scholarships Awarded to Girls in Rural Uganda

📅 July 10, 2026 ✍️ Programs Team ⏱️ 4 min read
📚

Fifty girls in Uganda started this term with something many of us take for granted: certainty that they'd finish school.

Last week, Musacope Foundation officially awarded 50 new scholarships to girls from rural communities in the Iganga, Mbale, and Kamuli districts. The scholarships cover full school fees, uniforms, books, sanitary supplies, and mentorship for the entire academic year.

Why Girls' Education Matters

In too many rural communities, girls are the first to drop out when families face financial pressure. A single medical bill, a bad harvest, or the arrival of a new baby can end a promising education overnight. And once a girl leaves school, the statistics are stark: earlier marriage, earlier motherhood, deeper poverty across generations.

Education flips those statistics on their head. Girls who finish secondary school earn more, marry later, have healthier children, and contribute more to their communities. It is one of the highest-return investments in the world — and one of the most joyful.

"When I heard I was chosen, I ran home and told my grandmother. She cried. I cried too. I want to become a doctor and come back and help our village."

— Sarah, 14, Iganga District

Who These Scholars Are

Our 50 new scholars were selected from over 200 applicants across three districts. Selection was based on need, academic promise, and community endorsement. They range from age 10 (upper primary) to age 17 (upper secondary). Each scholar is paired with a local mentor who checks in monthly and supports her through the year.

What the Scholarship Includes

  • Full school fees for the entire academic year
  • Uniforms and shoes
  • Textbooks, exercise books, and pens
  • A year's supply of menstrual hygiene products (a common reason girls miss school)
  • A trained local mentor for guidance and support
  • An annual medical check-up

The Cost of a Life-Changing Year

A full scholarship costs just $180 per year. That is less than what many of us spend on coffee in a month, and it is enough to keep a girl in school for a full academic year.

If you'd like to sponsor a scholarship — either fully or as part of a group — you can do so directly through our donation page. You can also send a message and we'll match you with a specific student to follow throughout the year.

Thank You

To every donor who made this possible: 50 girls, 50 families, and 50 futures thank you. This is what your generosity looks like on the ground.

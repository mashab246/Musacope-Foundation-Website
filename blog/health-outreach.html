Medical Outreach Reaches 3 New Villages in Karamoja

📅 July 3, 2026 ✍️ Health Team ⏱️ 4 min read
🏥

Over a single week in late June, our health outreach team travelled deep into Karamoja — one of Uganda's most remote and underserved regions — and delivered essential medical care to over 800 patients in three villages where no permanent health facility exists.

It was, quite simply, one of the most demanding and rewarding weeks in our foundation's history.

Why Karamoja?

Karamoja is home to some of the most resilient communities in East Africa — and also some of the most underserved. Nearest health facilities can be a full day's walk away. Malaria, respiratory infections, malnutrition, and untreated maternal complications remain leading causes of preventable illness and death.

For too many families here, "going to the doctor" simply isn't a realistic option. So we brought the doctors to them.

What We Delivered

Over 6 field days, our team of 12 clinicians, nurses, and community health volunteers provided:

  • 812 full consultations across three villages
  • 140 antenatal check-ups for expectant mothers
  • 210 pediatric consultations, including growth monitoring
  • 85 malaria treatments dispensed on-site
  • 60 referrals to district hospitals with follow-up coordination
  • 3 community health-education sessions on hygiene, nutrition, and malaria prevention
"The nurse gave me medicine for my baby and then sat with me for 20 minutes just explaining how to keep her healthy. Nobody has ever done that for me."

— Achen, mother of three, Kaabong District

A Moment That Stayed With Us

On day three, our team met a 62-year-old man named Lokeris who had walked more than 8 kilometers on a badly injured leg to reach the outreach camp. He hadn't seen a clinician in over 6 years. After cleaning and dressing the wound, our doctor arranged transport to the nearest district hospital, where he received proper treatment.

Small stories like Lokeris's are why we do this work. Not statistics. People.

The Cost of an Outreach Week

A full week of medical outreach in a remote area — including transport, medicines, supplies, and staff support — costs approximately $3,800. That's roughly $4.70 per patient reached. There are few better returns anywhere in global health.

What's Next

Our team is already planning the next round of outreach for August, expanding to two more villages we identified during this trip. If you'd like to support the next mission, your gift — of any size — directly funds medicines, transport, and the clinicians who make it possible.

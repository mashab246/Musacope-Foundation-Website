Pay with Card (Stripe)

You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout page. Cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay all accepted.

Pay with PayPal

Pay with your PayPal balance, bank account, or credit/debit card via PayPal.

Pay with Mobile Money

Supports MTN Mobile Money & Airtel Money (Uganda), M-Pesa (Kenya), plus mobile money across Ghana, Tanzania, Rwanda, and more via Flutterwave. You'll receive a prompt on your phone to approve the payment.

🔒 All payments are processed by secure, PCI-compliant providers. Musacope Foundation never sees your card or mobile money PIN.
